
 ### **EXAMINATION QUESTION** 

Within the 48-hour examination period, independently identify a software problem or opportunity of your choice and develop a functional, deployable software application using an appropriate software engineering lifecycle. 

Your work must demonstrate the application of requirements engineering, software effort estimation, analysis, design, implementation, testing, deployment, documentation, maintenance and future software evolution. 

Particular attention must be given to software effort estimation during requirements analysis and the identification, prioritisation and management of technical debt during implementation, testing and future maintenance. 

The project must be appropriately scoped for completion within 48 hours. You are not expected to develop a large commercial system; rather, you are expected to demonstrate sound and systematic Advanced Software Engineering practice. 

**PART A: EXAMINATION INSTRUCTIONS** 

## **1. Individual Work** 

- This is an individual end-of-semester examination. 

- You must independently select, analyse, design, implement, test and deploy your software project. 

- Collaboration on the development of another student's solution is not permitted. 

- You may discuss general software engineering concepts but must not share your project source code, SRS, design documents or completed solution with another student. 

## **2. Project Selection** 

- Select a realistic software problem or business opportunity. 

- The project may be related to business, education, healthcare, finance, agriculture, transportation, entertainment, public services or another appropriate domain. 

- Clearly define the problem and intended users. 

- Do not select a project that is too large to implement within 48 hours. 

- The project must contain enough functionality to demonstrate the software engineering principles covered in the course. 

## **3. Requirements Before Development** 

**Do not begin implementation immediately.** 

First: 

1. Define the problem. 

2. Identify stakeholders/users. 

3. Gather and analyse requirements. 

4. Define functional and non-functional requirements. 

5. Prioritise requirements. 

6. Estimate software effort. 

7. Define the scope of the system to be completed within the 48-hour period. 

Your requirements and effort estimation should guide your implementation decisions.
